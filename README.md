## About Me

I am **Muhammad Younas Khan**, also known as **Ahbab**, a BS Data Science student at [UET Mardan](https://uetmardan.edu.pk/) and the Founder and CEO of [AlgoHub SMC (Pvt) Ltd](https://algohubsmc.com).

I work at the intersection of software engineering, artificial intelligence, data science, and business process automation. My focus is on transforming practical problems into secure, scalable, and user-friendly digital products.

- Founder and CEO of **AlgoHub SMC (Pvt) Ltd**
- BS Data Science student at **UET Mardan**
- Developer of AI-powered, SaaS, web, and database-driven systems
- Interested in applied machine learning, automation, and digital transformation
- Exploring Microsoft Power Platform and Dynamics 365 solutions
- Passionate about open-source collaboration and student developer communities
- Based in Pakistan and open to international collaboration
